# Sky panorama

`babylon-sky-2048.webp` and `babylon-sky-4096.webp` are resized WebP derivatives
of **Kloppenheim 06 (Pure Sky)**, photographed by **Greg Zaal** and edited by
**Jarod Guest**, from Poly Haven.

- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky
- License: CC0 — https://polyhaven.com/license
- Source: Poly Haven's 8K tonemapped JPG, downloaded 12 September 2026.

This is an atmospheric sky asset, not documentary imagery of Babylon.
